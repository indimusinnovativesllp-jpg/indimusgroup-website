FindIt

FindIt is an innovative Lost & Found platform developed by Indimus Group. It connects people who have lost valuable items with those who have found them, making recovery faster, easier and more reliable.

Purpose

To provide a smart digital platform where users can report lost items, upload found items and reconnect owners with their belongings using modern technology.

Key Features

  • Report Lost Items
  • Upload Found Items
  • Advanced Search System
  • Image Upload Support
  • WhatsApp Contact Option
  • Secure User Interface
  • Mobile Friendly Design
  • Fast Search Experience
